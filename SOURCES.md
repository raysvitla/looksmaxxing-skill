# sources and method

## source corpus

This skill began as a structured reading of public QOVES Studio YouTube material, including regular videos and Shorts. The original collection ran in March 2026 and the local working index records **291 source videos**: 153 regular videos and 138 Shorts.

- QOVES Studio YouTube: <https://www.youtube.com/@QOVESStudio>
- QOVES website: <https://qoves.com>
- Topic index: [`reference/video-index.md`](./reference/video-index.md)

The topic index is a convenience map, not a complete bibliography and not proof for every sentence in the knowledge files. Where this repository makes a medical, psychological, or population-level claim, it should be checked against primary research or removed.

## what this repository adds

Ray Svitla authored the selection, structure, prompts, safety boundaries, summaries and task-oriented organisation in this repository. It does **not** claim ownership of third-party videos, transcripts, research, brands, images, or trademarks.

The material is kept as transformed notes for education and assistant use. Do not add verbatim transcripts, video downloads, copied thumbnails, or long quotations without the relevant permission.

## known limitations

- YouTube transcripts can be wrong, incomplete and decontextualised.
- A source creator discussing research is not the same as a systematic review or clinical guideline.
- Facial appearance is affected by lighting, camera lens, styling, culture, age, expression, health and personal taste.
- The repository is not medical, psychological, nutritional or surgical advice.

## how to improve a claim

When correcting or adding a claim, include:

1. the exact sentence or section;
2. a stable public source, preferably primary research or a clinical/professional guideline for medical topics;
3. what the source actually supports and what it does not;
4. whether the claim should be softened, qualified or removed.

Do not use a paper title as prestige wallpaper. If we cannot explain what it changes in practical advice, it probably does not belong in the skill.
