# Glasses Selection Guide

## Do Glasses Make You Less Attractive?

**Short answer:** It depends on the type of glasses, your face shape, and the context.

**Research findings:**
- **Stereotype:** Glasses reduce attractiveness but increase perceived intelligence
- **Reality:** Mixed results — some studies show no effect, others show specific frame styles enhance attractiveness
- **Context matters:** Professional settings (glasses boost competence perception), social settings (varies by style)

**The key:** Choosing the RIGHT glasses for YOUR face.

---

## The 3 Rules of Glasses Selection

### Rule 1: Frame Shape Opposes Face Shape

**Principle:** Create balance by choosing frames that contrast with your dominant facial feature.

| Face Shape | Best Frame Shapes | Avoid |
|-----------|------------------|-------|
| **Round** | Angular, rectangular, square | Round, oval |
| **Square** | Round, oval, cat-eye | Square, rectangular |
| **Oval** | Most shapes work | Oversized (can overwhelm) |
| **Heart** | Bottom-heavy, cat-eye, round | Top-heavy, decorative temples |
| **Oblong/Rectangle** | Deep frames, decorative temples | Narrow, small frames |
| **Diamond** | Cat-eye, oval, rimless | Narrow frames |

**Why this works:** Contrast softens dominant features and creates facial harmony.

---

### Rule 2: Frame Size Matches Face Size

**The Bridge Test:**  
- Frame width should align with (or be slightly wider than) your face at the temples
- Frames shouldn't extend past the widest part of your face
- Temples should sit flat without pinching

**Measurements that matter:**
1. **Lens Width:** 40-60mm (should cover eyes without extending far past outer corners)
2. **Bridge Width:** 14-24mm (should sit comfortably on nose without pinching or sliding)
3. **Temple Length:** 135-150mm (should reach ears without squeezing)
4. **Frame Width:** Should match face width at temples

**Pro tip:** Most frames have measurements printed on the inside of the temple (e.g., "52-18-140" = lens-bridge-temple).

---

### Rule 3: Consider Your Features

Beyond face shape, specific facial features affect what works:

**Eyebrows:**
- **Thick, dark brows:** Bold frames (complement strong features)
- **Thin, light brows:** Delicate frames (avoid overwhelming the face)
- **The "brow line" rule:** Top of frame should align with or sit just below your brows

**Nose:**
- **Wide nose:** Bridge with wider nose pads or keyhole bridge (creates space)
- **Narrow nose:** Standard bridge or adjustable nose pads
- **Long nose:** Low bridge or decorative bridge (breaks up length)

**Cheekbones:**
- **High cheekbones:** Cat-eye frames (accentuate them)
- **Low cheekbones:** Rectangular or oval (add definition)

**Skin tone & Coloring:**
- **Warm undertones** (gold jewelry looks best): Tortoise, gold, warm browns, honey, olive
- **Cool undertones** (silver jewelry looks best): Black, silver, blue, purple, gray
- **Neutral undertones:** Most colors work

## Frame Styles Explained

### 1. Rectangular / Wayf

arer
**Best for:** Round, oval faces  
**Effect:** Adds angularity, creates structure  
**Vibe:** Classic, professional, versatile  
**Examples:** Ray-Ban Wayfarer, rectangular plastic frames

---

### 2. Round / Circular
**Best for:** Square, rectangular faces  
**Effect:** Softens angular features  
**Vibe:** Vintage, creative, intellectual  
**Examples:** John Lennon glasses, P3 frames

---

### 3. Cat-Eye
**Best for:** Heart, square, oval faces  
**Effect:** Lifts and widens upper face, adds femininity  
**Vibe:** Retro, glamorous, bold  
**Examples:** 1950s-style cat-eye, upswept frames

---

### 4. Aviator
**Best for:** Oval, oblong, heart faces  
**Effect:** Balances proportions, adds edge  
**Vibe:** Cool, casual, military-inspired  
**Examples:** Tear-drop aviators, metal double-bridge

---

### 5. Browline / Clubmaster
**Best for:** Oval, oblong, triangle faces  
**Effect:** Emphasizes brows, adds retro sophistication  
**Vibe:** Intellectual, vintage, distinguished  
**Examples:** Ray-Ban Clubmaster

---

### 6. Geometric / Hexagonal
**Best for:** Round, oval faces  
**Effect:** Adds modern edge, creates interest  
**Vibe:** Contemporary, fashion-forward  
**Examples:** Hexagon metal frames

---

### 7. Rimless / Semi-Rimless
**Best for:** Any face shape (minimal design)  
**Effect:** Barely-there, emphasizes natural features  
**Vibe:** Professional, minimalist, understated  
**Examples:** Frameless titanium, nylon wire frames

---

## Material Guide

### Plastic / Acetate
**Pros:** Bold, colorful, fashion-forward, lightweight  
**Cons:** Can break, less adjustable  
**Best for:** Statement style, casual wear

### Metal (Titanium, Stainless Steel)
**Pros:** Durable, adjustable, professional  
**Cons:** Can look generic if poorly designed  
**Best for:** Professional settings, everyday wear

### Wood / Horn
**Pros:** Unique, eco-friendly, luxury feel  
**Cons:** Expensive, requires care  
**Best for:** Fashion statements, high-end style

### Mixed Materials (Acetate + Metal)
**Pros:** Combines best of both (e.g., browline frames)  
**Cons:** More complex, higher cost  
**Best for:** Versatile, retro-modern style

## Lens Considerations

### Clear Lenses
- Everyday prescription or blue-light blocking
- Professional, neutral

### Tinted / Colored Lenses
- Fashion statement
- Can make eyes less visible (reduces face-to-face connection)

### Photochromic (Transitions)
- Darken in sunlight
- Convenient but can look odd indoors during transition

### Blue Light Blocking
- Yellow/amber tint (filters blue light from screens)
- May have slight color cast

**Pro tip:** Clear lenses with anti-reflective coating are most versatile for daily wear.

## How to Try Glasses (Virtual & In-Person)

### In-Person:
1. **Check proportions:** Frame width should match face width
2. **Profile check:** Frames shouldn't extend far past your cheeks
3. **Comfort:** No pinching at nose or temples, no sliding
4. **Eye position:** Eyes should be centered in the lens (vertically and horizontally)
5. **Take photos:** Front, side, 3/4 views (sometimes what looks good in the mirror doesn't photograph well)

### Virtual Try-On:
- Use apps (Warby Parker, Zenni, EyeBuyDirect)
- Take measurement of current glasses if you have them
- Order "try at home" kits
- Screenshot and compare side-by-side

## Face Shape-Specific Recommendations

### Round Face

**Goal:** Add angles, elongate

**Best frames:**
- Rectangular
- Square
- Geometric (hexagon, octagon)
- Cat-eye (for women)

**Avoid:**
- Round, circular frames (emphasize roundness)
- Small frames (get lost on face)

**Examples:** Warby Parker Percey, Ray-Ban RX5228

---

### Square Face

**Goal:** Soften angles, add curves

**Best frames:**
- Round, oval
- Cat-eye (softens jaw)
- Browline (balances strong jawline)

**Avoid:**
- Square, rectangular (too harsh)
- Boxy frames

**Examples:** Ray-Ban Round Metal, Oliver Peoples Gregory Peck

---

### Oval Face

**Goal:** Maintain balance (most versatile face shape)

**Best frames:**
- Almost anything works
- Rectangular, round, cat-eye, aviator all work

**Avoid:**
- Oversized frames (can overwhelm)
- Frames that are too small

**Examples:** Any classic style

---

### Heart Face

**Goal:** Balance wide forehead, narrow chin

**Best frames:**
- Bottom-heavy frames (add width to lower face)
- Cat-eye (but not too wide at top)
- Round, oval
- Rimless

**Avoid:**
- Top-heavy, decorative temples (emphasize forehead)

**Examples:** Cat-eye, aviators

---

### Oblong / Rectangle Face

**Goal:** Add width, reduce length

**Best frames:**
- Deep frames (tall lenses)
- Decorative or wide temples
- Aviators, oversized round

**Avoid:**
- Narrow, small frames (elongate face further)
- Thin, wire frames

**Examples:** Oversized round, bold rectangular

---

### Diamond Face

**Goal:** Soften angles, balance wide cheekbones

**Best frames:**
- Cat-eye (widens upper face)
- Oval
- Rimless

**Avoid:**
- Narrow frames (emphasize cheekbone width)

**Examples:** Cat-eye, delicate metal frames

## Color Selection

### Skin Tone Matching:

**Warm Undertones:**
- Best: Tortoise, honey, gold, olive, warm browns, cream
- Avoid: Cool-toned grays, silvers, black

**Cool Undertones:**
- Best: Black, silver, blue, purple, gray, translucent
- Avoid: Warm oranges, golds, honey

**Neutral Undertones:**
- Best: Almost any color (most versatile)
- Trend toward classic: Black, tortoise, clear

**Hair Color Considerations:**
- **Dark hair:** Bold colors (black, tortoise, bright colors)
- **Light hair:** Softer tones (tortoise, clear, pastels, metals)
- **Red hair:** Greens, blues, tortoise (complement red tones)

**Eye Color:**
- **Blue eyes:** Brown, tortoise, gold frames (contrast)
- **Brown eyes:** Green, blue, purple (complement)
- **Green eyes:** Purple, burgundy, tortoise (enhance)

## Glasses for Specific Contexts

### Professional / Work Settings

**Best:**
- Rectangular or oval frames (balanced, serious)
- Neutral colors (black, tortoise, silver)
- Metal or conservative acetate
- Rimless or semi-rimless (subtle)

**Perception boost:** Intelligence, competence, trustworthiness

**Avoid:** Overly trendy, colorful, or oversized frames

---

### Casual / Social Settings

**Best:**
- Bold, colorful frames (express personality)
- Trendy styles (cat-eye, geometric, colored lenses)
- Mix of materials

**Effect:** Fashion-forward, creative, approachable

---

### Dating / Attractiveness

**Research suggests:**
- **Men:** Rimless or subtle frames (avoid "nerdy" stereotype)
- **Women:** Cat-eye or bold frames (enhance features, signal confidence)
- **Both:** Well-fitted, clean, modern styles

**Avoid:** Outdated styles, ill-fitting frames, overly intellectual looks (if goal is attractiveness over perceived intelligence)

---

## Common Mistakes

1. **Choosing frames based on trends, not face shape**
2. **Ignoring frame size** (too big or too small)
3. **Not considering your coloring** (frame color clashes with skin tone)
4. **Skipping the profile check** (frames stick out too far)
5. **Wearing outdated styles** (replace every 2-3 years)
6. **Dirty or scratched lenses** (instantly ruins the look)
7. **Not adjusting frames regularly** (loose, crooked frames look sloppy)
