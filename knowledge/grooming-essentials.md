# Grooming Essentials

## Skincare Fundamentals

### The Basic Routine
1. **Cleanser** (AM + PM)
2. **Moisturizer** (AM + PM)
3. **Sunscreen** (AM only, SPF 30+)

### Advanced Options
- **Retinoids** (anti-aging, acne treatment)
- **Chemical exfoliants** (AHAs, BHAs for texture)
- **Serums** (vitamin C, hyaluronic acid, niacinamide)

## Facial Hair (Men)

### Stubble (Most Attractive)
- 5-7 days growth
- Signals maturity without being "too much"
- Keep edges clean (cheek and neck lines)

### Full Beard
- Can enhance weak jawlines
- Must be groomed (trim, shape, condition)
- Avoid patchiness (if patchy, stick to stubble)

### Clean-Shaven
- Best for showing off strong features
- Youthful appearance
- Professional contexts

## Eyebrow Grooming

### Men
- **Keep thick** (masculinity signal)
- **Clean up:** Remove unibrow, trim long hairs
- **Don't over-pluck:** Avoid thin or arched brows

### Women
- Shape to complement face
- Avoid over-tweezing (thick brows are in)
- Use brow gel or powder for fullness

## Hair Care

### Quality > Length
- Grow as long as you can maintain quality
- Regular trims prevent split ends
- Use heat protection when styling

### Hair Loss Prevention
- **Minoxidil** (Rogaine) — topical treatment
- **Finasteride** (Propecia) — prescription oral medication
- Start early (easier to maintain than regrow)
