# Style & Fashion Principles

## How to Find Your Skin Undertone

Your undertone determines which colors (clothing, accessories, makeup) look best on you.

### The Tests:

**1. Vein Test:**
- Look at the veins on the inside of your wrist (natural lighting)
- **Green veins** = Warm undertone
- **Blue/purple veins** = Cool undertone
- **Blue-green/can't tell** = Neutral undertone

**2. Jewelry Test:**
- **Gold jewelry** looks better = Warm
- **Silver jewelry** looks better = Cool
- Both look good = Neutral

**3. White Paper Test:**
- Hold white paper next to your face (natural lighting, no makeup)
- **Yellow/golden glow** = Warm
- **Pink/rosy glow** = Cool
- **Gray/olive** = Neutral (or olive undertone)

**4. Sun Reaction:**
- **Tan easily** = Usually warm
- **Burn easily** = Usually cool

## Color Theory for Clothing

### Warm Undertones

**Best colors:**
- Earth tones: camel, beige, brown, terracotta
- Warm reds, oranges, yellows
- Olive green, moss green
- Coral, peach, salmon
- Cream, ivory (not stark white)
- Gold accents

**Avoid:**
- Icy pastels (baby blue, lavender)
- Stark white, black (can look harsh)
- Cool blues and purples

**Metals:** Gold, bronze, copper

---

### Cool Undertones

**Best colors:**
- Jewel tones: emerald, sapphire, ruby, amethyst
- True white, black, navy
- Cool blues, purples, pinks
- Gray (charcoal, light gray)
- Burgundy, wine
- Icy pastels

**Avoid:**
- Orange, yellow-greens
- Warm browns (camel, tan)
- Gold tones

**Metals:** Silver, platinum, white gold

---

### Neutral Undertones

**Best colors:**
- Almost everything works!
- Lean toward muted, mid-tones
- Soft whites, taupes, grays

**Metals:** Both gold and silver work

## Seasonal Color Palettes

### Spring (Warm, Light, Bright)
- **Characteristics:** Warm undertone, light hair, bright eyes
- **Colors:** Peach, coral, light aqua, warm greens, camel, ivory
- **Metals:** Gold

### Summer (Cool, Light, Muted)
- **Characteristics:** Cool undertone, light to medium coloring, soft features
- **Colors:** Soft blues, lavenders, rose pink, light grays, navy
- **Metals:** Silver, rose gold

### Autumn (Warm, Deep, Muted)
- **Characteristics:** Warm undertone, rich hair color (auburn, dark brown), hazel/brown eyes
- **Colors:** Rust, olive, mustard, terracotta, burgundy, chocolate brown
- **Metals:** Gold, bronze

### Winter (Cool, Deep, Bright)
- **Characteristics:** Cool undertone, high contrast (dark hair + light skin OR deep skin tone)
- **Colors:** True white, black, royal blue, emerald, fuchsia, icy pastels
- **Metals:** Silver, platinum

## Dressing for Your Body Type

### Men:

**Athletic (V-shape):**
- **Strengths:** Broad shoulders, narrow waist
- **Goal:** Maintain proportions
- **Best:** Fitted shirts, tailored jackets, slim/straight pants
- **Avoid:** Oversized tops (hide physique), skinny jeans (too bottom-heavy)

**Slim (Ectomorph):**
- **Goal:** Add dimension, avoid looking frail
- **Best:** Layering, textured fabrics, horizontal stripes, fitted (not tight)
- **Avoid:** Baggy clothes, vertical stripes

**Heavy/Stocky:**
- **Goal:** Elongate, streamline
- **Best:** Vertical lines, monochrome, structured jackets, dark colors
- **Avoid:** Horizontal stripes, tight clothes, light colors on problem areas

**Short:**
- **Goal:** Add height
- **Best:** Monochrome, vertical lines, higher-rise pants, avoid cuffs
- **Avoid:** Contrast breaks (different color top/bottom), baggy clothes

**Tall:**
- **Goal:** Break up height
- **Best:** Layering, contrast breaks, cuffed pants, horizontal elements
- **Avoid:** Monochrome head-to-toe, too-short sleeves/pants

---

### Women:

**Hourglass:**
- **Characteristics:** Bust and hips similar width, defined waist
- **Best:** Belted waists, wrap dresses, fitted styles
- **Avoid:** Shapeless, boxy cuts

**Pear (Triangle):**
- **Characteristics:** Hips wider than bust
- **Best:** A-line skirts, boat necks, embellished tops, dark bottoms
- **Avoid:** Skinny jeans with plain tops, horizontal stripes on bottom

**Apple (Inverted Triangle):**
- **Characteristics:** Shoulders/bust wider than hips
- **Best:** V-necks, A-line dresses, bootcut pants, darker tops
- **Avoid:** Shoulder pads, strapless tops, tight tops + skinny bottoms

**Rectangle:**
- **Characteristics:** Bust, waist, hips similar width
- **Best:** Belts, peplum, ruffles (create curves)
- **Avoid:** Straight, shapeless dresses

## Fit & Tailoring (Most Important)

**The Rule:** Fit > Brand > Price

### Men's Fit Guide:

**Shirts:**
- Shoulders: Seam sits at edge of shoulder (not drooping or tight)
- Chest: Can fit 2-3 fingers between chest and fabric
- Length: Covers belt when arms are down
- Sleeves: End at wrist bone (when arms hang)

**Pants:**
- Waist: Snug without belt (can fit 2 fingers)
- Rise: No excessive fabric bunching at crotch
- Thigh: Slim but not tight (can pinch fabric)
- Break: Slight break (pants touch shoes), or no break (cropped)

**Suits:**
- Jacket shoulders should align with your shoulders
- Sleeve shows 1/4-1/2 inch of shirt cuff
- Jacket length: Covers your butt, ends mid-zipper

### Women's Fit Guide:

**Dresses:**
- Shoulders align (not drooping)
- Bust: No gaping or pulling
- Waist: Define waist (unless intentionally oversized)
- Length: Personal preference, but hemlines should be intentional

**Pants:**
- Waist: Sits comfortably (no muffin top)
- Hips: Smooth, no pulling
- Length: Depends on shoe (heels = longer OK, flats = cropped or slight break)

**The #1 Rule:** Tailor inexpensive clothes rather than buying expensive ill-fitting ones.

## Accessories

### Watches (Men):

**Face size by wrist:**
- **Small wrist (<6.5"):** 38-42mm case
- **Medium wrist (6.5-7"):** 40-44mm
- **Large wrist (>7"):** 44-48mm

**Strap color:**
- **Warm undertone:** Brown leather, gold
- **Cool undertone:** Black leather, silver
- **Formal:** Leather strap or metal bracelet
- **Casual:** Canvas, NATO strap

---

### Jewelry:

**Necklace length:**
- **Short neck:** Longer chains (avoid chokers)
- **Long neck:** Chokers, shorter chains work

**Ring size:**
- Proportional to hand size
- Avoid oversized rings on small hands

**Bracelets:**
- Should fit snugly but not tight (can fit one finger between wrist and bracelet)

## Style Archetypes

### Minimalist
- **Palette:** Neutrals (black, white, gray, beige)
- **Cuts:** Clean, simple lines
- **Brands:** COS, Uniqlo, Everlane
- **Vibe:** Sleek, modern, effortless

### Classic / Preppy
- **Palette:** Navy, white, pastels
- **Pieces:** Button-downs, chinos, blazers, loafers
- **Brands:** Ralph Lauren, J.Crew, Brooks Brothers
- **Vibe:** Polished, traditional, Ivy League

### Streetwear
- **Palette:** Bold colors, logos
- **Pieces:** Hoodies, sneakers, joggers, oversized fits
- **Brands:** Supreme, Off-White, Nike, Adidas
- **Vibe:** Urban, trendy, casual

### Rugged / Workwear
- **Palette:** Earth tones, denim, leather
- **Pieces:** Boots, flannels, denim, canvas
- **Brands:** Carhartt, Red Wing, Levi's
- **Vibe:** Masculine, utilitarian

### Bohemian
- **Palette:** Earth tones, patterns, textures
- **Pieces:** Flowing fabrics, layering, natural materials
- **Brands:** Free People, Anthropologie
- **Vibe:** Artistic, free-spirited

## Common Mistakes

1. **Ignoring fit** (buying based on brand or price, not how it fits)
2. **Wearing wrong colors** (clashing with undertone)
3. **Too much matching** (avoid head-to-toe same color unless intentional monochrome)
4. **Outdated styles** (holding onto clothes from 10+ years ago)
5. **Over-accessorizing** (less is more)
6. **Ignoring shoe quality** (cheap, worn shoes ruin an outfit)
7. **Not tailoring** (inexpensive tailored clothes > expensive ill-fitting ones)
