# Style Principles

## Dressing for Your Face & Body

### Face-Focused Principles
- **Round face:** V-necks, vertical lines (elongate)
- **Angular face:** Rounded necklines (soften)
- **Long face:** Horizontal elements, wide collars

### Body Type Basics
- **Tall:** Can wear almost anything
- **Short:** Vertical lines, monochrome, avoid heavy contrast
- **Athletic:** Tailored fits show off physique
- **Slim:** Layering adds dimension

## Color Theory

### Skin Undertones
- **Warm:** Gold jewelry, earth tones, warm colors
- **Cool:** Silver jewelry, jewel tones, cool colors
- **Neutral:** Both work, but avoid extremes

### Seasonal Palettes
- **Spring:** Warm, light, bright
- **Summer:** Cool, light, muted
- **Autumn:** Warm, deep, muted
- **Winter:** Cool, deep, bright

## Accessories & Face Shape

### Glasses
- **Round face:** Angular frames
- **Square face:** Round/oval frames
- **Long face:** Wide frames
- **Small face:** Small, delicate frames

### Jewelry
- Complements skin tone (gold vs. silver)
- Proportion to body size
