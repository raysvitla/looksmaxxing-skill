# Common Mistakes

## Hairstyle Mistakes

### 1. Copying Celebrity Hairstyles Without Context
- **Mistake:** "I want Jennifer Aniston's hair"
- **Why it fails:** Her hairstyle works for *her* features, not necessarily yours
- **Fix:** Analyze your 6 features first, then find styles that work for them

### 2. Hiding Your Strengths
- **Mistake:** Covering a great jawline with long hair, or hiding a perfect hairline with bangs
- **Fix:** Identify your best features and show them off

### 3. Ignoring Hair Quality
- **Mistake:** Growing hair longer than it can remain healthy
- **Fix:** Cut to the length where quality is still high

### 4. Symmetrical Hair on Asymmetrical Faces
- **Mistake:** Center parts and perfectly even styles when you have facial asymmetry
- **Fix:** Use asymmetrical styles (side parts, off-center bangs)

## Grooming Mistakes (Men)

### 1. Over-Plucking Eyebrows
- **Mistake:** Making brows thin or overly arched
- **Why it fails:** Thick brows = masculinity
- **Fix:** Clean up unibrow and long hairs, but keep them thick

### 2. Patchy, Unkempt Beards
- **Mistake:** Growing a beard when it's too patchy or not grooming it
- **Fix:** If patchy, stick to stubble. If full, groom it (trim, shape, condition)

### 3. Ignoring Body Fat
- **Mistake:** Focusing on exercises or grooming while staying at high body fat
- **Fix:** Lose fat first — it reveals all your features

### 4. Mouth Breathing
- **Mistake:** Chronic mouth breathing (leads to poor jaw development and weak facial structure)
- **Fix:** Breathe through your nose, practice proper tongue posture (mewing)

## Style Mistakes

### 1. Ignoring Skin Undertone
- **Mistake:** Wearing colors that clash with your undertone
- **Fix:** Identify if you're warm, cool, or neutral, then choose colors accordingly

### 2. Wearing the Wrong Glasses
- **Mistake:** Choosing frames that emphasize your face's extreme features
- **Fix:** Round face → angular frames; square face → round frames

## Aesthetic Myths

### 1. "Mewing Will Transform My Face"
- **Reality:** Mewing (tongue posture) may help during development (teens), but won't drastically change adult faces
- **Better:** Lose body fat, improve posture, consider orthodontics if needed

### 2. "Chewing Gum Builds Jawline"
- **Reality:** Minimal effect; can overdevelop masseter muscles (makes face wider, not more angular)
- **Better:** Lose body fat

### 3. "Face Shape Determines Everything"
- **Reality:** Individual features (hairline, jawline, symmetry) matter more than generic "face shape"
- **Better:** Use the 6 features framework
