# Facial Proportions

## The Rule of Thirds

The face is divided into three equal vertical sections:
1. **Upper third:** Hairline to eyebrows
2. **Middle third:** Eyebrows to bottom of nose
3. **Lower third:** Bottom of nose to chin

**Ideal:** All three sections are roughly equal in height.

## The Rule of Fifths

The face is divided horizontally into five equal sections (each the width of one eye):
1. Outer edge to outer corner of eye
2. Outer corner to inner corner of eye (one eye width)
3. Between eyes (one eye width)
4. Inner corner to outer corner of other eye (one eye width)
5. Outer corner of eye to outer edge

**Ideal:** Each section is equal.

## Golden Ratio (Phi = 1.618)

Applied to facial features:
- Face length to width ratio: ~1.618
- Eye-to-lip distance to nose-to-chin distance: ~1.618
- Mouth width to nose width: ~1.618

**Note:** The golden ratio is an *ideal*, not a requirement. Many attractive faces deviate from it.

## Key Insights

- **Youthful proportions:** Larger upper third, smaller lower third (childlike features)
- **Masculine proportions:** Larger lower third (jaw + chin prominence)
- **Feminine proportions:** Balanced thirds, larger eyes relative to face
